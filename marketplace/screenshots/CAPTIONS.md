# Marketplace screenshots

Taken from the real product on the development site, 1440 × 900 (device scale 2×),
Statamic 6.31 control panel. Suggested captions, in upload order:

1. `01-dashboard-widget.png` — The dashboard widget: your overdue pages, the site's, and the thirty-day trend.
2. `02-lifecycle-overview.png` — The Lifecycle screen: every page's state, by collection and by owner, each number a way into the list.
3. `03-content-list.png` — The content list, filtered to overdue: owner and how sure, the date, the last review.
4. `04-entry-panel.png` — The entry sidebar: owner, interval, review-by, exemption, and the Review panel with Mark reviewed.
5. `05-settings.png` — Settings: the owner of last resort per site and an escalation contact per collection.
6. `06-bulk-actions.png` — Bulk actions on any entry listing: assign owner, mark reviewed, set interval, exempt.

Not in the shots: the governance report (console / PDF) and the reminder email. The listing's
usage example covers the report; see LISTING.md.
